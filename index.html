RESPECT THE FREQUENCY

RULES

Entry and ID

Your name must be on the guestlist. Guests of an invitee must also be on the list. Keep your ID with you, as you may be asked to show it.

Prohibited items

Not permitted: weapons of any kind, hazardous materials, hard drugs and illicit substances.

Conduct
  • Treat all attendees, DJs, staff, and organizers with respect.
  • Discrimination, bullying, intimidation, threats, and violence will not be tolerated.
  • Follow venue regulations, safety instructions, and staff directions.
  • Do not damage venue property, equipment, or personal belongings.
  • Keep the venue clean and dispose of rubbish responsibly.
  • Do not interfere with emergency exits, safety equipment, or event operations.
  • Notify event staff of medical emergencies, safety hazards, or situations requiring assistance.
  • Violations may result in refusal of entry, removal from the event, or a ban from future events.
Anti-harassment rules
  • Harassment, stalking, bullying, and intimidation are strictly prohibited.
  • Unwanted sexual advances, persistent flirting after rejection, and inappropriate comments are unacceptable.
  • Discriminatory behavior or remarks based on gender, sexuality, race, religion, disability, or background will not be tolerated.
  • Do not retaliate against anyone who reports harassment or seeks assistance.
  • Report incidents to an event organizer or staff member. Reports will be taken seriously and handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.
  • Depending on the severity of the incident, appropriate action may include removal from the event or exclusion from future events.
Consent rules
  • Always obtain consent before touching someone or initiating intimate contact.
  • Consent must be freely given, clear, and mutual. Silence or lack of resistance does not constitute consent.
  • Respect a person’s refusal immediately, without pressure, persuasion, or repeated advances.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time, and all activity must stop immediately when it is withdrawn.
  • Someone who is unconscious, incapacitated, or too impaired to make an informed decision cannot give valid consent.
  • Attending the event, dancing, dressing a certain way, or being friendly does not imply consent.
  • Obtain permission before photographing or filming identifiable attendees, and never share intimate or sensitive images without consent.
Photography, filming and social media
  • By attending the event, you acknowledge that photography and video recording may take place throughout the event.
  • Your image, likeness, or appearance may be captured and used by Freedom Frequencies for promotional, marketing, and advertising purposes across its website and social media platforms.
  • You may capture and share your own photos and videos, and don’t forget to tag @freedom_frequencies.wav on Instagram so we can share your moments and keep the frequency alive!
  • Please respect other attendees’ privacy and personal boundaries when taking or sharing photos and videos, particularly in sensitive situations.
Re-entry

Exit and re-entry are possible. Wristbands must be intact.
